§1 PROBLEM STATEMENT (ONE LINE)
================================================================================

tvrootshell can spawn uid=0 dt_helper and trustcache /var/jb binaries, but
processes still hit Seatbelt op-16 (file-map-executable) deny on /var/jb dylibs
because no sandbox **extension tokens** are attached to the slot-0 profile.

Dopamine solves this with launchdhook issuing tokens + systemhook consuming them
in every spawned process — tvrootshell has neither layer.
